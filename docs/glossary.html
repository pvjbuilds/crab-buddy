What the crab does

TermWhat it isSay it likeLives in
moodWhat Claude is doing, as one of 25 ids (think, read, build, test…). Tool calls and turn events pick it.“the test mood”Mood in types · classify(), step(), shown() in crab.ts
lookWhat the band shows right now: a mood, one of its takes and the caption. A switch is the look changing.“the look switches too often”look atom · show() in register.tsx
takeOne version of a mood. Take 0 carries the mood's plain name (think), the others a number (think1 to think8). Busy moods have 9, each build scene 2, the rest 4. See the atlas below.“think5 is my favourite; drop read7”take 0: LOOK · others: TAKE · CSS .cb-think5 · count: takes()
sceneThe 14 s a take plays for: work, a beat, more work, a second beat. Then the next take.“scene length; a longer scene”SCENE in crab.ts and 14s in scene(), art.ts: change both
work loopThe take's own short motion, repeating inside the scene: the hammer swing every 4 s, the saw every 0.5 s.“think5's work loop is too fast”the take's CSS rules, .cb-<take> in art.ts
beatA short break where the whole crab moves: hop, admire, turn, stretch, shimmy or tilt. Two per scene, at about 4.8–6.2 s and 10.1–11.5 s. A take always gets the same pair, picked from its id.“bigger beats; one beat per scene; no turn beat”BEATS, WINDOW, scene() in art.ts
emoteThe tiny icon over the crab's shoulder during a beat: bang (!), note (♪), spark, ask (?), love (heart), sweat (drop).“no hearts on test; bigger emotes”EMOTE in art.ts
glum moodstestf, debug, dizzy, long, ask. Their emotes are only sweat, ask or bang, never a heart.“make long glum too”GLUM in art.ts
propA thing on the crab's body or claw: magnifier, flask, coffee cup.“bigger magnifier”first slot of a LOOK/TAKE entry · .cb-pr
setA thing standing beside the crab: the wall, laptop, easel, gears.“move the laptop closer”second slot ("beside") of a LOOK/TAKE entry
claw heightWhere the front claw rests for a take (14 is resting).“raise the claw”third slot of a LOOK/TAKE entry
build sceneOne of the five moods a build rotates through, 14 s each: build (hammer and wall), saw, type (laptop), paint, gear.“swap the order of build scenes”BUILDS in crab.ts
lapOne pass through the five build scenes (70 s). Lap 2 plays each scene's take 1: weld, drill, blueprint, sweep, crane. So 10 scenes, 140 s, before one repeats.“a third lap”take() in crab.ts
walk-inThe sideways scuttle from where the crab stands to the new look's station. The whole stage takes 4.8 s; never under 0.8 s.“faster walk-in”walkFor() in art.ts
roamA slow drift about the station on a long loop: idle 80 s, think 24 s, read 32 s, web 12 s, done 10 s, long 40 s. Paint walks right once.“idle roams too far”ROAM in art.ts
stage extrasStage-wide effects behind the crab: the brick wall (build), paint stripe (paint), confetti (fixed), footprints (walk-in), the trip (testf). Hidden under reduce motion.“more confetti; no footprints”stageArt(), .st-fx
walking takesTakes that walk their legs the whole time: inst, codex, think3, done2.“make read walk”WALKS in art.ts
terminal crabThe block-character crab in a terminal. One look per mood, a frame every 150 ms. No takes, scenes, beats or stage there.“the terminal crab”term.tsx · LOOKS, FRAME

Easy to mix up

mood vs look vs take

A mood is what Claude is doing. A take is one way the crab acts it. The look is the mood and take on screen right now.

scene vs build scene

Every take plays as a 14 s scene. A build scene is one of the five moods a build rotates through (build, saw, type, paint, gear).

beat vs work loop

The work loop is the take's own repeating motion. A beat is the break from it, twice per scene, the same six moves for every take.

prop vs set vs stage extra

A prop is on the crab, a set stands beside it, a stage extra spans the whole stage behind it.

dwell vs hold vs scene

Dwell (8 s): the least a look stays. Hold (5 s): how long a result shows. Scene (14 s): how long one take plays before the next.

animation

Too vague on its own. Say which: the take, its work loop, a beat, an emote, the walk-in or a stage extra.

How to ask for a change

Name the part, then the mood or take, then the change. One term is enough to find the code.

SayWhat changes
Make think5's work loop slower.One take's CSS rule: .cb-think5 in art.ts.
Make the beats bigger, or keep one beat per scene.BEATS or WINDOW in art.ts.
Dwell 5 s instead of 8.DWELL in register.tsx.
Move read's station left.STATION.read in art.ts.
Add five one-liners to git.LINES.git in crab.ts.
A new take for web: a fishing rod.A TAKE entry, its CSS and the count in takes().
A new mood for a deploy.The Mood type, classify(), MEANING, LINES, STATION, LOOK and term.tsx.
